Food & Cuisine

A royal feast for the senses — the legendary tastes of Hyderabad

Must Try Dishes

Iconic Hyderabadi Food

These legendary dishes define Hyderabad's culinary identity

Signature Dish

Hyderabadi Biryani

The crown jewel of Hyderabadi cuisine. Fragrant basmati rice layered with slow-marinated meat, whole spices, caramelised onions and saffron-infused milk — cooked in the legendary dum (sealed-pot) method for several hours. Available as Kacchi (raw meat) or Pakki (pre-cooked meat) style.

📍 Best At: Paradise, Bawarchi, Shah Ghouse, Shadab

GI Certified

Hyderabadi Haleem

A rich, slow-cooked stew of wheat, barley, lentils and tender meat — cooked for up to 12 hours until everything blends into a thick, velvety consistency. Garnished with crispy fried onions, fresh ginger, lime and coriander. Hyderabadi Haleem holds a GI tag — a first for any meat product in India.

📍 Best At: Shah Ghouse, Pista House, Hotel Shadab

City Staple

Irani Chai

The soul of Hyderabad — a thick, milky, sweetened tea simmered for hours in a tawa-like vessel, creating an incredibly rich and layered brew. Best enjoyed with Osmania biscuits at one of the city's traditional Irani cafes, a cultural tradition that has existed for over 150 years in the city.

📍 Best At: Nimrah Cafe (Charminar), Grand Hotel, Café 555

Heritage Snack

Osmania Biscuit

Named after the last Nizam, Mir Osman Ali Khan, this buttery, slightly sweet and salty biscuit is a Hyderabadi classic. Its crumbly, melt-in-the-mouth texture makes it the perfect companion for Irani Chai. The original recipe from the royal kitchens has been preserved for over 100 years.

📍 Best At: Irani cafés across the Old City

Royal Dessert

Double Ka Meetha

A decadent Hyderabadi bread pudding made from deep-fried bread slices soaked in sweetened, reduced milk infused with saffron and rose water, then garnished lavishly with dry fruits, pistachios and silver leaf. A royal dessert passed down from the Nizam's court — rich, indulgent and unforgettable.

📍 Best At: Shadab, Baig's, Hyderabad House

Festival Sweet

Qubani Ka Meetha

A classic Hyderabadi dessert made from dried apricots (qubani) soaked and simmered with sugar into a syrupy, jammy compote. Traditionally served at weddings and festivals, it is enjoyed warm or cold, often with a dollop of cream or custard for extra richness.

📍 Best At: Traditional wedding caterers, Hyderabadi restaurants

Classic Starter

Hyderabadi Kebabs

From Shami and Pathar (stone-grilled) kebabs to Boti and Seekh, Hyderabadi kebabs are a class apart. Marinated in complex spice blends and slow-cooked over charcoal, they deliver unmatched depth of flavour. The Pathar Gosht — thin meat cooked on a heated stone slab — is a Hyderabadi invention.

📍 Best At: Hotel Shadab, Bade Miyan, Paradise

Summer Favourite

Falooda & Lassi

Hyderabad's Falooda is a tall, beautiful glass layered with rose syrup, vermicelli noodles, milk, basil seeds and topped with ice cream — a refreshing treat beloved in summer. Alongside it, the rich, thick Hyderabadi Namkeen Lassi and the fruity Mango Lassi are city icons served chilled at numerous street stalls.

📍 Best At: Charminar area, Mozamjahi Market
