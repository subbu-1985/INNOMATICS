National Museum  
★★★★★

Salar Jung Museum

One of the three national museums of India, Salar Jung Museum houses the personal collection of Salar Jung III — a lifetime of passionate global acquisitions containing over 40,000 rare artifacts. Highlights include the mesmerising Veiled Rebecca marble sculpture (which appears transparent), a room of rare clocks including the famous double-sided clock, ancient manuscripts, miniature paintings, and a stunning collection of European and Indian art across 38 galleries.

Royal Museum  
★★★★★

Nizam's Museum

Housed in the beautiful Purani Haveli palace, the Nizam's Museum showcases the personal belongings and treasures of the last Nizam of Hyderabad, Mir Osman Ali Khan. Collections include the world's largest wardrobe (containing 14,718 suits), rare cutlery, priceless jewellery, gifts from dignitaries, antique cars, and a stunning golden cradle. The museum provides an intimate glimpse into the extraordinary life of one of history's richest men.

Science Museum  
★★★★★

Birla Science Museum

Established by the Birla Foundation in 1985, this engaging science museum makes science fun and accessible for all ages. The museum features galleries on dinosaurs (including a real Rajasaurus skeleton), space exploration, mathematics, nuclear physics, a 3D theatre, and interactive exhibits. Adjacent to the Birla Planetarium, it offers both educational and entertaining experiences for families and students.

Unique Museum  
★★★★★

Sudha Cars Museum

One of the most extraordinary and quirky museums in the world, Sudha Cars Museum showcases over 100 fully functional car models built in unusual shapes — including a giant computer, camera, cricket bat, hamburger, high heel shoe, toilet seat and even a pool table! Engineer K Sudhakar has spent decades handcrafting these vehicles, earning multiple Guinness World Records. An absolute must-visit attraction that is both hilarious and awe-inspiring.

State Museum  
★★★★☆

Telangana State Archaeology Museum

Located in the Public Garden complex, this historic museum was established in 1930 and is one of the oldest in south India. The museum houses extensive collections of prehistoric artifacts, ancient coins, sculptures from various dynasties (Kakatiyas, Satavahanas, Qutb Shahis), inscriptions, manuscripts and arms. The Egyptian mummy and navagraha sculptures are among the notable attractions.

Planetarium  
★★★★★

Birla Planetarium

Adjacent to Birla Science Museum, the Birla Planetarium takes visitors on an immersive journey through the cosmos with its state-of-the-art projection system and domed theatre. Regular sky shows in Telugu and English present exciting narratives about planets, stars, galaxies and space exploration. The sky show is a perfect experience for families, students and astronomy enthusiasts alike.
