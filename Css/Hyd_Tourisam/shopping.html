Shopping in Hyderabad

From royal bazaars to modern malls — retail therapy at its finest

Traditional Markets

Famous Bazaars

Step into Hyderabad's centuries-old markets brimming with heritage and craftsmanship

Heritage Market

Laad Bazaar (Choodi Bazaar)

The most famous market in Hyderabad, located next to Charminar, renowned for its spectacular lac bangles (choori), pearl sets, semi-precious stones, ittar (perfume) and wedding accessories.

  • Best for lac bangles, pearl jewellery and wedding attire
  • Bargain politely — vendors expect negotiation
  • Visit on weekdays to avoid weekend crowds
📍 Old City, near Charminar
★★★★★
Wholesale Hub

Begum Bazaar

The largest wholesale market in Hyderabad, known as the backbone of the city's commerce. An enormous market where traders source dry fruits, spices, groceries, hardware, textiles and electronics in bulk.

  • Great for dry fruits, spices and wholesale goods
  • Open from 7AM — go early for best bargains
  • Chaotic but extremely affordable prices
📍 Begum Bazaar Road
★★★★☆
Commercial Hub

Abids

Hyderabad's iconic commercial district — a blend of modern shops and old-world charm. Famed for books, electronics, garments, handicrafts and the famous Abids Book Market that draws bibliophiles from across the city.

  • Must-visit for book lovers and electronics
  • Great mix of branded and local stores
  • Easy TSRTC bus and metro connectivity
📍 Abids, Hyderabad
★★★★☆
Garment Market

Koti Market

A bustling commercial area famous for a wide variety of clothing, textiles, cosmetics and accessories at street-level prices. A favourite destination among students and budget-conscious shoppers.

  • Affordable clothing, cosmetics and accessories
  • Great for readymade garments and fabrics
  • Many government and corporate buildings nearby
📍 Koti, Hyderabad
★★★★☆
Modern Retail

Malls & Shopping Centres

World-class malls with premium brands, entertainment and dining

Premium Mall

GVK One Mall

An upscale mall in Banjara Hills featuring international luxury brands, fine dining restaurants, a multiplex cinema and a plush food court. One of Hyderabad's first premium shopping destinations.

Luxury Cinema Fine Dining
Family Mall

Inorbit Mall

A massive family-friendly mall in Cyberabad featuring over 100 stores, a food court with diverse cuisines, entertainment zone, Hamleys toy store and major fashion brands across multiple floors.

Family 100+ Stores Food Court
Mega Mall

Sarath City Capital Mall

One of the largest malls in India, spread across 7 floors with 300+ stores including international brands, India's first urban entertainment theme park, IMAX theatre and multiple dining options.

300+ Stores IMAX Theme Park
Swedish Giant

IKEA Hyderabad

Hyderabad was home to IKEA's first India store — India's largest furniture showroom. Features 1,000+ affordable home furnishing solutions, a Swedish restaurant serving traditional meatballs, and a parking for 1,000 cars.

Furniture Restaurant First in India
Lifestyle Mall

Forum Sujana Mall

A lifestyle destination in Kukatpally featuring branded retail, hypermarket (Big Bazaar), multiplex and a vibrant food court. Popular with families and young shoppers in western Hyderabad.

Lifestyle Multiplex Food Court
Nexus Mall

Nexus Mall

Located in the heart of the city near Hyderabad Central, this mall offers a solid mix of fashion brands, electronics stores, beauty salons, gaming zones and a well-stocked food court.

Central Location Fashion Gaming
