Top Attractions

The most loved destinations among travellers visiting Hyderabad

★ 4.8

Charminar

Built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, Charminar is the iconic symbol of Hyderabad. This magnificent mosque and monument stands at the heart of Old City, drawing millions of visitors every year.

⏰ 9AM–5:30PM 💰 ₹25 (Indian) 📍 Old City
★ 4.7

Golconda Fort

A grand medieval fortress that was once the seat of the Qutb Shahi dynasty, known for its legendary diamond trade, impressive acoustics and the famous sound and light show held every evening.

⏰ 8AM–5:30PM 💰 ₹25 (Indian) 📍 Ibrahim Bagh
★ 4.6

Ramoji Film City

The world's largest integrated film studio complex, recognised by the Guinness World Records. Sprawling across 2,000 acres with hotels, sets, gardens, rides and daily live entertainment shows.

⏰ 9AM–7PM 💰 ₹1150+ 📍 Hayathnagar
★ 4.5

Hussain Sagar Lake

A stunning heart-shaped lake built in 1563, connecting Hyderabad and Secunderabad. Famous for the 18-metre tall rock statue of the Buddha, boat rides and the vibrant Necklace Road promenade.

⏰ Open 24hrs 💰 Free Entry 📍 Tank Bund
★ 4.6

Salar Jung Museum

One of the three national museums of India, housing Salar Jung III's personal collection of 40,000+ rare artifacts from across the world, including the famous Veiled Rebecca marble sculpture.

⏰ 10AM–5PM 💰 ₹20 (Indian) 📍 Dar-ul-Shifa
★ 4.5

Birla Mandir

A pristine white marble temple dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, built on a hilltop offering panoramic views of the city. The intricate carvings and architecture blend North and South Indian styles magnificently.

⏰ 7AM–9PM 💰 Free Entry 📍 Naubath Pahad
