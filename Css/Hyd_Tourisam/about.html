About Hyderabad

Four centuries of royal legacy, culture and unstoppable progress

Our Story

The City That Never Stops Amazing

Hyderabad, the capital of Telangana state, is a city where the splendour of its Nizami past coexists perfectly with the dynamism of its tech-driven present. Founded by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in 1591, the city was originally built on the banks of the Musi River.

The Nizams of Hyderabad, who ruled from 1724 to 1948, were once among the richest rulers in the world — their passion for architecture, art and culture gave the city its magnificent monuments, language, cuisine and traditions. Today, Hyderabad is also the fourth largest technology hub in the world and home to global giants like Microsoft, Google, Amazon and Apple's largest India campuses.

Timeline

History of Hyderabad

1591 AD
Foundation of Hyderabad
Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah founds Hyderabad on the southern bank of the Musi River, naming it after Hyder Ali, a local saint.
1591–1687
Qutb Shahi Dynasty
Seven rulers of the Qutb Shahi dynasty built the Golconda Fort, Charminar, Mecca Masjid and the Qutb Shahi Tombs. The city became a world diamond trading centre.
1724 AD
The Nizam Era Begins
Mir Qamar ud-Din Khan establishes the Asaf Jah dynasty. For over 200 years the Nizams rule Hyderabad as India's wealthiest and most powerful princely state.
1948 AD
Integration with India
After Indian independence, Operation Polo led to Hyderabad's integration into the Indian Union on 17 September 1948, ending the Nizam's rule.
1990s–2000s
Rise of Cyberabad
Hyderabad transforms into a major IT hub. HITEC City is developed, attracting Microsoft, Oracle, Google and dozens of Fortune 500 companies, earning the nickname "Cyberabad."
2014 – Present
Capital of Telangana
Hyderabad becomes the capital of the newly formed Telangana state. The city continues to grow as one of India's fastest developing metro cities.

Getting Around

Transportation Options

Hyderabad has excellent connectivity within the city and with the rest of India

✈️
By Air

Rajiv Gandhi International Airport with connections to 60+ cities in India and 50+ international destinations. Located 22 km from city centre.

🚅
By Train

Secunderabad Junction is the main railway hub with high-speed trains to Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bangalore and all major Indian cities.

⚡
Metro Rail

Hyderabad Metro Rail covers 3 lines and 57 stations. Fast, clean and affordable — the best way to navigate the city. Runs 6AM to 10PM daily.

🚌
TSRTC Buses

TSRTC operates 3,500+ buses covering all areas. The bus network is extensive and the most affordable way to travel across the city.

Prepare Your Trip

Essential Travel Tips

Insider advice to help you make the most of your Hyderabad visit

🌡️

Dress for the Weather

Light cotton clothes are ideal. Carry a shawl for temples and mosques which require modest dress and head coverings.

🚗

Use Ride-Sharing Apps

Ola and Uber are widely available and affordable. For Old City, shared autos are faster than cabs due to narrow lanes.

💳

Digital Payments Accepted

UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe) is accepted almost everywhere. Keep some cash handy for street markets and small vendors.

🍽️

Try the Street Food

Street food is generally safe at busy stalls. Must-tries: Irani Chai at Nimrah Cafe, biryani at Bawarchi, haleem at Pista House.

📸

Photography Permits

Photography is allowed at most sites. A few temples restrict cameras inside the sanctum. Always ask before photographing people.

🕐

Plan Around Closing Days

Most museums and the Salar Jung Museum are closed on Fridays. Plan your museum visits accordingly to avoid disappointment.

🛍️

Bargain at Bazaars

At traditional markets like Laad Bazaar and Begum Bazaar, prices are negotiable. Start at 50-60% of the quoted price.

🌐

Buy a Local SIM

Affordable Indian SIM cards (Jio, Airtel, Vi) provide excellent 4G/5G coverage across Hyderabad. Available at the airport.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are needed to see Hyderabad? +
A minimum of 3–4 days is recommended to visit the major attractions comfortably. To explore Old City, museums, parks, and nearby sites, plan for 5–7 days. Golconda Fort and Ramoji Film City each deserve a full day.
Is Hyderabad safe for solo travellers and families? +
Hyderabad is generally considered safe for tourists, including solo women travellers and families. Exercise standard precautions in crowded markets. The city has a strong police presence in tourist areas. Emergency number: 100.
What is the best area to stay in Hyderabad? +
Banjara Hills and Jubilee Hills offer premium accommodations and great restaurants. For the heritage experience, stay near the Old City. Madhapur/HITEC City is ideal for business travellers. Secunderabad is convenient for rail travellers.
Is the Hyderabadi Biryani really that special? +
Absolutely! Hyderabadi Biryani is widely considered one of the finest biryanis in the world. The unique dum technique, specific spice blends, and saffron-infused rice create a flavour profile that cannot be replicated elsewhere. Try it at Paradise, Bawarchi, or Baig's.
Where can I buy authentic Hyderabadi pearls? +
Laad Bazaar near Charminar is the most famous place for pearls and lac bangles. For certified quality, visit established jewellers in Abids, Banjara Hills or Begum Bazaar. Look for Basheerbagh Pearl Market for wholesale and quality pearls at competitive prices.
