Temple Etiquette

Visitor Guidelines

👟

Remove Footwear

Always remove shoes before entering any temple premises

👔

Dress Modestly

Wear clothes that cover shoulders and knees out of respect

📵

Silent Phones

Keep mobile phones on silent; photography may be restricted

🕐

Avoid Peak Hours

Visit on weekday mornings to avoid large crowds

🌸

Prasad & Flowers

Offering flowers and prasad is encouraged and welcomed

🤫

Maintain Silence

Keep conversations to a minimum and respect the sacred space
